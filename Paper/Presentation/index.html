1 Delta-KV Compression

Compresses the key and value matrices along the sequence dimension before QK multiplication.

Applies Delta compression on top of a block-sparse method (XAttention).

Uses adjacency structure as a tile importance estimator for block-sparse attention, choosing which tiles to compute.

DPXA

Apply Delta packing on Q and K separately. Multiply the packed Q̂ and K̂ to get representative attention scores in tiles. Sum the tile attention scores to get a tile score.

  • K̂ holds 10 anchors, compression ratio is a = 10/16 = 0.625.
  • Q̂ holds 8 anchors, compression ratio is b = 8/16 = 0.5.
  • The scoring ratio is a × b : (0.625) × (0.5) = 31.25%.

Conclusion: Delta-KV & DeltaXAttention

1 Delta-KV Compression

  • Competitive RULER average (89.6 vs 91.5 for full attention) at 50% KV reduction, up to 1.35 speedup.
  • Dominated by existing work on speed and accuracy.
  • Demonstrates the potential of delta packing.

2 DeltaXAttention

  • Halves the KV operations of XAttention by trading some accuracy (-3 vs XAttention) .
  • No realized speedup

Conclusion: DPXA — No Silver Bullet

Shape-oriented
Excel in speedup

MInference

  • Realizes its speedup late.
  • Llama RULER long-context degradation (63.73 vs 74.46).

FlexPrefill

  • Struggles with model generalization.
  • Llama LongBench degradation (44.78 vs 47.53).
Block-sparse
Excel in accuracy retention · limited speedup

XAttention

  • Excels on Qwen, falls back on Llama
  • High scoring cost.

  • Low scoring cost.

Future Work, Strengths & Limitations

Future Work

  • Separate thresholds for Q and K, and per-layer / per-head thresholds.
  • Test tile-score normalization schemes.

Strengths

  • Fair comparison: every method run from its own codebase with default config.
  • Covers the strong players in the training-free sparse-attention acceleration arena.
  • All speedups measured end-to-end, so no method benefits from a cherry-picked kernel metric.

Limitations

  • No InfiniteBench.
  • Only understanding tasks evaluated; video and image generation left untested.
